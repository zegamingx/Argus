## 5. SPADA Indonesia: program-scoped CC BY discovery path

The official 2022 Program Bantuan Inovasi Modul Digital (IMD) announcement states that modules/content produced under that program must be licensed Creative Commons CC BY and made openly accessible through SPADA.

This does **not** mean every SPADA course, module or file has CC BY permission. The announcement applies to outputs of that specific funded program. The announcement page does not itself identify every completed module, so no individual SPADA title is activated by this document.

Discovery procedure:
1. Identify an exact module and its program/cohort provenance.
2. Verify that it was produced under the 2022 IMD program.
3. Capture the individual module's author, institution, publication record and license notice.
4. Confirm that the downloaded file is the same item as the licensed record.
5. Preserve attribution and checksum.
6. Review third-party figures, excerpts, references and embedded resources.
7. Assign retrieval and training permissions separately.

Evidence: [Official SPADA — Program Bantuan Inovasi Modul Digital (IMD) Tahun 2022](https://spada.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/berita/program-bantuan-inovasi-modul-digital-imd-tahun-2022).

## 6. Coverage and limitations

The additional ITATS candidates improve Indonesian technical, information-systems, business-process, entrepreneurship and project-management coverage. They do not provide broad Indonesian language competence or a balanced national knowledge corpus.

Still missing from this identified CC BY source group:
- Indonesian grammar, linguistics, reading and writing foundations.
- Indonesian literature and cultural history.
- Mathematics and natural-science textbooks in Indonesian.
- Geography, environmental science and regional studies.
- History, archaeology, civics and comparative social sciences.
- Health, agriculture, food science and practical knowledge.

A single university program's OER repository is a useful seed source, not a substitute for national and regional diversity. Discovery must seek additional institutions, disciplines, languages and regional perspectives.

## 7. Retrieval and training policy

For every item:
- Store the individual landing page and catalog row as evidence.
- Store the exact file URL and SHA-256 checksum.
- Preserve author, institution, title, year, language, type and attribution.
- Inspect the PDF for third-party figures, tables, code, quotations and standards excerpts.
- Record parser version, page count, extraction warnings and actual tokenizer token count.
- Keep retrieval permission separate from training eligibility.
- Keep training disabled until the item-specific rights review and training acceptance gates pass.

The CC BY 4.0 label is a favorable rights signal for reuse with attribution, but it does not automatically prove that every embedded component is original or that every intended model-training operation has been legally cleared.
